Loadouts & Certificates Guide

Loadout slots, accessory stat modifiers, and every stat rollable from Ripped, Wooden, and Premium Certificates of Authenticity.

Stat Modifiers

Accessories you wear can roll bonuses that change how you play. Green stats are buffs that help you; red stats are nerfs that work against you. Matching stats stack across everything you wear, and grading an accessory makes its buffs and passives stronger.

StatEffect
LuckRaises the value of items found in auctions, safes, and fishing; boosts average garage value.
Energy Drink TimeEnergy drink luck lasts longer.

WalkspeedFaster walking speed.
Vehicle SpeedFaster vehicle top speed.
Bid RecoveryRecover faster after a missed bid.
Bid Zone WidthWidens the green zone on the bid bar.
Bid Arrow SpeedSlows the bid cursor for easier stops.
NPC Offers BonusNPC shoppers offer more for items sold.

Certificates of Authenticity

Basic Authentication Stats

Standard bonuses from any Ripped and Wooden Certificate Of Authenticity except a Premium Certificate (which only ever rolls a Premium Authentication), regardless of slot:

  • Bid Recovery
  • Bid Arrow Speed
  • Bid Zone Width
  • Tip Chance
  • NPC Offers Bonus
  • Walkspeed
  • Energy Drink Time — luck bonus lasts longer while worn.

Premium Authentications

Trade a Wooden Certificate Of Authenticity and 150 Diamonds at the Premium Certificates NPC in Lucky Bay to unlock the stats below on an accessory.

AuthenticationSlotEffect
Club PactWristAdds Luck shared by every member of your active club while equipped; contributions from different members stack.
DroughtbreakerBackAt Luck Limit 100%, each auction without a mutation adds Luck to the next, stacking up to 5x. Resets on a mutation appearing; inactive while Luck is lowered.
Golden HourBackGradually gains Luck while equipped — 2%/min at max (as low as ~0.5%/min, unconfirmed), capping at +100%. Pauses when removed; rejoining within 20 min keeps progress.
Mutated LuckHeadGrants extra Luck from the worn accessory's strongest mutation.
Temporal SplitWristChance for an item to instantly gain a Time Capsule mutation when placed in the Time Capsule.
